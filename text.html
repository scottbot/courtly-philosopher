The Book

Barros’s little book, which “moralizes” the sheet: the text of the Naples edition of 1588 (the one issued with the surviving board), transcribed from the only copy, in Vienna, with an English translation beside it. Choose an edition to see the passages that differ in the Madrid editions of 1587.

The little book has eight parts. Most readers will want the walk through the board, and the rules.

  1. The title page
  2. The king’s license to print
  3. The approval, by the poet Alonso de Ercilla
  4. Sonnets by Liñán de Riaza and Cervantes
  5. Barros to the reader: why he wrote it
  6. Dedication to Mateo Vázquez
  7. The walk through the board, square by square
  8. How the game is played
